Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Laugh** ($2): write a single
funny line, a joke about anything at all, capped at 140 characters. A judge
model reads the entries two at a time and picks the funnier one, round after
round, until the funniest line is left standing.

The program in `main.ts` assembles the entry from labeled beats (setup, turn,
punchline), validates it against the gate (1–140 characters, one line, not
only whitespace), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> My plants died, so I bought plastic ones — now even the fake ones look disappointed in me.
